From a rough idea to a plan you can actually follow

Four stages, usually spread over a week of evenings. Most groups reach a settled plan somewhere in stage three.

Walk through the full process
  1. 01

    Tell us the shape of the trip

    Dates, budget band, who is coming and how fast you like to move. Two minutes of input, not a questionnaire.

  2. 02

    Waypost drafts the route

    A day-by-day skeleton with realistic travel times between stops, and the nights you should not be moving.

  3. 03

    Everyone edits together

    Your travel party swaps stops, comments on the costs and votes on the two options nobody can agree about.

  4. 04

    Take it with you

    Offline day cards, tickets and directions on the phone in your pocket, updating as plans shift on the road.

Questions people ask before they start

Does Waypost book flights and hotels?

No. Waypost is planning software, not a travel agency or a booking platform. You book with whichever airline, hotel or operator you prefer, then keep the confirmation inside your plan so the whole trip sits in one place.

Where do the price estimates come from?

Destination figures on this site are indicative ranges based on typical mid-range travel costs for the season shown. They are a starting point for budgeting, not quotes, and they do not include airfare from your home city. Actual prices vary with dates, availability and how you book.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Yes, on paid plans. Download your trip before you travel and the day cards, directions and stored tickets stay available offline. Anything you change offline syncs the next time you have a connection.

What happens to my plans if I stop paying?

Your account drops to the free Compass plan. Every trip you have made stays readable and exportable indefinitely. You will not be able to create new trips beyond the free limits until you subscribe again.

Do you sell my travel data?

No. We do not sell or rent personal data to anybody, and we do not use your trip contents to target advertising. Our privacy policy sets out exactly what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.
