1. General information only

Everything published on waypost.travel is provided for general information. It is not professional advice and should not be treated as a substitute for checking with the relevant airline, operator, embassy, insurer or authority before you commit money or travel.

2. Prices and estimates

Destination budgets, trip lengths, seasonal windows and travel times shown on this site are indicative estimates, based on typical mid-range costs at the time of research. They are not quotes and they are not offers.

  • Figures exclude airfare from your home city unless a page says otherwise.
  • Actual costs vary with your dates, party size, how far ahead you book, exchange rates and local demand.
  • Travel times assume normal conditions. They do not account for strikes, closures, weather or seasonal timetable changes.
  • Where we cannot verify something with reasonable confidence, the product says so rather than guessing.

3. Not a travel service

Waypost Travel Technologies, Inc. is a software company. We are not a travel agency, tour operator, transport provider, accommodation provider or insurer, and we are not an agent for any of them.

We do not sell, book, reserve or issue any travel product, and we take no payment on behalf of any travel supplier. When you book something, that contract is between you and the supplier. Their cancellation terms, refund rules and liabilities apply, and we have no ability to influence them on your behalf.

4. Safety, visas and health

We do not provide visa, immigration, customs, insurance, medical or vaccination advice, and nothing on this site should be read as such. Entry requirements change frequently and vary by nationality.

Before you travel, check the current U.S. Department of State travel advisory and CDC health notice for your destination, along with the entry rules published by the country you are visiting, and hold appropriate travel insurance. It is your responsibility to ensure you meet every entry requirement that applies to you.

5. Accuracy and currency

Destination research is reviewed quarterly, and we correct errors quickly when we find them or when they are reported. Even so, opening hours change, routes close, operators go out of business and prices move. We do not warrant that any information on this site is complete, accurate or current at the moment you read it.

6. External links

This site links to third-party websites for your convenience. We do not control them, we are not responsible for their content, accuracy, availability or privacy practices, and a link is not an endorsement.

7. Editorial independence

We take no commission, referral fee or payment from any hotel, airline, operator or booking platform mentioned on this site. Nothing in our destination library or guides is a paid placement, and there are no affiliate links. The company is funded by subscriptions from the people using the software.

8. Testimonials

Quotes attributed to travelers on this site are drawn from real feedback given to us with permission, and are edited only for length and clarity. They describe the experience of individual users. They are not a promise that you will have the same experience, and they are not a guarantee of any outcome.

9. Photography

Photographs on this site are licensed stock images used to illustrate destinations and moods. They may not depict the exact accommodation, viewpoint, route or conditions you will encounter, and are not representations about any specific supplier or property.

10. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, we accept no liability for loss or damage arising from reliance on information published on this site, including missed transport, canceled bookings, denied entry, closures, additional cost or lost time. The limits set out in clause 12 of our terms and conditions apply.

Nothing here excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be excluded. If you are a consumer, your statutory rights are unaffected.

11. Report an error

If something on this site is wrong, tell us and we will fix it. Email hello@waypost.travel with the page and what is inaccurate. Corrections to destination data are usually published within a week.
