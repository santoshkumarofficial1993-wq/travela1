1. Who we are

This site and the Waypost planning service are operated by Waypost Travel Technologies, Inc., a corporation registered in Delaware, United States (EIN 88-3914027) with its registered office at 1550 Wewatta Street, Suite 400, Denver, Colorado 80202, United States.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "Waypost" mean that company. "You" means anyone who visits this website or uses the Waypost service. We are the data controller for the personal data described below.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. Your name, email address, password (stored only as a cryptographic hash) and, optionally, a profile photo and home city.
  • Trip content. Destinations, dates, notes, budgets, uploaded booking confirmations and anything else you add to a plan, along with the names or email addresses of people you invite to it.
  • Billing details. Billing name, billing address, tax exemption certificate if you supply one, and the last four digits and expiry of your payment card. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Correspondence. Messages you send through the contact form, by email or by phone, including anything you choose to include in them.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and connection data. IP address, browser type and version, operating system, screen size and referring page.
  • Usage data. Pages viewed, features used, timestamps and error reports. This is what tells us which parts of the planner people get stuck on.
  • Cookies and similar storage. Described in section 6 and in our cookie policy.

We do not ask for and do not want special category data such as health, religion, political opinion or biometric information. Please do not put such details into trip notes.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your account, and to provide the planning service you signed up for.
  • To build, save, sync and share your trip plans with the people you invite.
  • To take payment, issue invoices and meet tax obligations.
  • To respond to support requests, refund requests and other correspondence.
  • To keep the service secure, detect abuse and prevent fraud.
  • To understand which features are used and where the product fails, so we can improve it.
  • To send service messages you cannot opt out of while you hold an account, such as billing notices, security alerts and material changes to these terms.
  • To send the monthly planning note, only if you have asked for it, and only until you unsubscribe.

We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone. We do not use the contents of your trips to target advertising, and we do not share trip contents with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Legal grounds for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation or comparable law applies to you, we rely on the following grounds:

PurposeGround
Running your account and providing the servicePerformance of a contract
Billing, invoicing and tax recordsLegal obligation and contract
Security, fraud prevention and product improvementLegitimate interests
Optional analytics cookies and marketing emailConsent, which you may withdraw at any time

If you are a resident of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or another US state with a comprehensive consumer privacy law, that law gives you the rights described in section 8. We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we have never done so.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run Waypost, and only to the extent they need it. Each is bound by contract to protect it and to use it for no other purpose.

CategoryWhat they receive
Cloud hosting and databaseAll service data, stored encrypted
Payment processingBilling name, email, amount, card token
Transactional and marketing emailName and email address
Error and performance monitoringDevice data, error traces, account identifier
Customer support toolingCorrespondence and account identifier

We also share data with the people you invite to a trip, which is the point of a shared plan. We will disclose data to a public authority where the law requires it, and to an acquirer if the company is sold, in which case this policy continues to apply until you are told otherwise.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your theme and cookie choices. These cannot be switched off without breaking the site.

We also use optional analytics cookies to measure aggregate usage. These are set only if you accept them in the cookie notice. Declining them changes nothing about the service you receive. Full detail, including names and lifetimes, is in the cookie policy.

8. Your rights

Subject to the law that applies to you, you may ask us to:

  • confirm what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we have no overriding legal reason to keep it;
  • restrict or object to a particular use, including profiling;
  • provide your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave, without affecting what was done before you withdrew it.

Export is available directly from your account settings. For anything else, write to privacy@waypost.travel. We respond within 30 days and will tell you if we need longer and why. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest on our hosting provider's infrastructure. Access to production systems is limited to staff who need it, protected by two-factor authentication, and logged. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes and cannot be recovered by us or by anyone else.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and creates a risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

10. International transfers

Our primary infrastructure is hosted in the United States and the European Union. Some service providers named in section 5 operate elsewhere. Where personal data leaves your country, we rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent lawful transfer mechanism, and we assess the provider's safeguards before we use them.

11. Children

Waypost is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. Adults may of course include children as travelers on a trip. If you believe a child has registered an account, tell us at privacy@waypost.travel and we will remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the way we handle data changes. The date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects your rights, we will email account holders at least 14 days before it takes effect.
