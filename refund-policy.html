Refund and cancellation policy

Cancel from your settings at any time. New subscriptions are refundable in full for 14 days. Everything else is spelled out below.

1. What this covers

This policy applies to subscriptions to the Waypost planning service bought from this website by consumers and businesses. It forms part of our terms and conditions.

It does not cover travel bookings. Waypost does not sell flights, hotels, tours or tickets, and takes no payment on behalf of travel suppliers. If you want to cancel a booking, that is between you and the supplier you booked with, under their cancellation rules.

2. Canceling a subscription

  • Cancel at any time from Settings, then Billing, then Cancel subscription. No email, phone call or retention conversation is required.
  • Cancellation stops the next renewal. It does not end your current period, and you keep full access until that period expires.
  • After it expires, the account moves to the free Compass plan. Nothing you have created is deleted.
  • If you cannot reach your settings for any reason, email billing@waypost.travel from the address on the account and we will cancel it for you.

3. The 14-day refund window

If you are unhappy with a new subscription, tell us within 14 days of the first payment and we will refund it in full. You do not need to give a reason, and we will not ask you to justify it.

This applies to your first paid subscription on an account, and to the first payment after a genuine upgrade between plans. It does not reset each renewal.

4. Refunds after 14 days

SituationWhat you get
Yearly plan canceled within 14 days Full refund of the yearly charge
Yearly plan canceled after 14 days Pro-rata refund of complete unused months, on request
Monthly plan canceled after 14 days No refund for the current month, access continues to period end
Renewal you did not intend, reported within 7 days and unused Full refund of that renewal
Duplicate or incorrect charge Full refund of the excess, always
Extended outage that materially prevented use Pro-rata credit or refund for the affected period
Feature removed that materially reduces what you paid for Pro-rata refund for the unused period

5. What is not refundable

  • Periods already used on a monthly plan, beyond the 14-day window.
  • Partial months on a yearly plan. Pro-rata refunds count complete unused months only.
  • Accounts closed by us for a serious breach of the terms and conditions.
  • Taxes already remitted to the authorities, where the law does not permit us to recover them. In practice this is rare and we will tell you if it applies.
  • Third-party costs you incurred elsewhere, such as flights, hotels or non-refundable tickets.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Email billing@waypost.travel from the address registered on the account.
  2. Include the invoice number or the date and amount of the charge.
  3. Tell us briefly what happened. This is for our own product notes, not a test you have to pass.

We acknowledge every refund request within one business day and tell you the outcome within three. If we decline a request, we will explain why in writing and point to the clause we relied on.

7. How long refunds take

Payment methodTypical time to appear
Credit or debit card5 to 10 business days after approval
ACH bank transfer3 to 5 business days after approval
Apple Pay or Google Pay5 to 7 business days after approval

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method. We cannot send a refund to a different card, account or person. Timing after we approve a refund is controlled by your bank, not by us.

8. Failed and disputed payments

If a payment fails, we retry over 14 days and email you each time. If it still fails, the account moves to the free plan and your trips stay readable.

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank. We will almost always resolve it faster. Accounts with an open chargeback are suspended until it is settled.

9. Price changes

We give existing subscribers at least 30 days' notice of any price increase. A change never applies to a period you have already paid for. If you do not want to continue at the new price, cancel before the renewal date and you will not be charged.

10. Your data after canceling

Canceling does not delete anything. Every trip you have built stays readable and exportable on the free plan for as long as your account exists. If you want the account and its data deleted entirely, ask at privacy@waypost.travel and we will action it within 30 days, as described in the privacy policy.
