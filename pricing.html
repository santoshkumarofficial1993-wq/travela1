How billing works, in plain terms

  • Currency and tax. All prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax. Any state or local sales tax that applies to your billing address is calculated and shown before you confirm.
  • When you are charged. Monthly plans bill on the same date each month. Yearly plans bill once, on the day you subscribe, and again on that date the following year.
  • Renewals. Subscriptions renew automatically so your trips do not lock mid-journey. We email you seven days before any yearly renewal.
  • Canceling. Cancel from your account settings at any time. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, then move to the free Compass plan.
  • Refunds. New subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days. Full detail is in the refund and cancellation policy.
  • Payment methods. Credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and ACH bank transfer, processed by our payment partner. Waypost never stores your full card details.

No hidden charges. There is no setup fee, no per-traveler fee and no commission on anything you book elsewhere. If a price on this page is ever out of date, the price shown at checkout is the one that applies.

Billing questions

Is there a free trial of the paid plans?

There is no timed trial. The Compass plan is free permanently, and the 14-day refund window on new paid subscriptions serves the same purpose without a countdown.

Can I switch plans partway through a cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged the difference for the remaining period. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current cycle, so you keep what you paid for.

Do my travel companions need to pay?

No. Anyone you invite to a trip can view, edit and comment for free. They only need a plan of their own if they want to organize their own trips.

What happens to my trips if I cancel?

Every trip you have created stays readable and exportable on the free plan for as long as your account exists. You will not lose the record of trips you have already taken.

Do you offer student or non-profit pricing?

Yes, 50% off Voyager for full-time students and registered non-profits. Contact us with proof of status and we will apply it to your account.
