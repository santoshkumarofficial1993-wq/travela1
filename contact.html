Before you write in

Three things that answer most of what reaches our inbox.

I want a refund

New subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days, no questions asked. Email billing@waypost.travel from the address on the account. The refund and cancellation policy covers every other case.

I want my data exported or deleted

Export is available from your account settings at any time. For deletion, write to privacy@waypost.travel and we will action it within 30 days, as set out in the privacy policy.

I want you to book something for me

We cannot. Waypost is planning software and is not a travel agency, tour operator or booking service. We do not sell travel, take payment for travel, or act as an agent for any supplier. You book directly, and keep the confirmation in your plan.
