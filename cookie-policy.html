1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store. Local storage works similarly but stays on your device rather than travelling with each request. This policy covers both, along with anything else that keeps state in your browser.

We divide them into two groups: the ones the site cannot work without, and the ones you choose.

2. Strictly necessary

Set on every visit. These carry no advertising identifiers and are never shared with third parties.

KeyTypePurposeLifetime
waypost-theme-v2Local storageRemembers if you switched to the dark theme. Absent until you use the toggleUntil you clear it
waypost-cookie-choiceLocal storageRemembers your answer to the cookie notice so we stop asking12 months
wp_sessionCookieKeeps you signed in to the planner30 days, or until sign out
wp_csrfCookieProtects forms against cross-site request forgeryBrowser session

Because these are strictly necessary to deliver a service you asked for, they do not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive or comparable law. Blocking them will sign you out and reset your theme on every page.

3. Optional analytics

Set only if you choose Accept all in the cookie notice. They tell us which parts of the planner people abandon, in aggregate.

KeyTypePurposeLifetime
wp_aCookieDistinguishes one browser from another so a visit is not counted twice13 months
wp_a_sCookieGroups page views into a single session30 minutes

Analytics data is aggregated, IP addresses are truncated before storage, and nothing in it is used to build an advertising profile. Declining changes nothing about the service you receive.

4. What we do not use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking pixels or social media trackers.
  • No fingerprinting to identify you without storage.
  • No selling or sharing of any of this with data brokers.

If that ever changes, this page changes first and the cookie notice will ask you again before anything is set.

5. Third-party requests

Some resources on this site load from other domains. They do not set cookies here, but the request itself reaches those providers and includes your IP address:

ProviderWhat it serves
Google FontsThe typefaces used across the site
unpkgThe icon font used for interface icons
UnsplashDestination and editorial photography

6. Changing your choice

Clear the site data for waypost.travel in your browser and the notice will appear again on your next visit, letting you choose differently. Signed-in users can also change the setting under Settings, then Privacy.

7. Browser controls

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and site data. Look under Settings, then Privacy and security, in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge or Brave. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in.

We honour Global Privacy Control signals where your browser sends them, and treat them as a decline of optional analytics.
